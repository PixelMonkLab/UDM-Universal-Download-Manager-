3 · How to use it

  1. Open a YouTube video, Instagram reel or Pinterest pin and click the purple ⬇ Download button at the bottom-right.
  2. On other sites, play the video. The VDM icon shows a number. Open VDM and click Download next to the video found.
  3. Right-click any page or link → Download video with VDM.
  4. Shortcuts: Alt+Shift+V opens VDM, Alt+Shift+D downloads the video on the page.
