==============================================================
  VDM - Video DOWNLOADS Manager   v1.0 Beta
  HOW TO USE (MAC)
==============================================================


--------------------------------------------------------------
 FIRST TIME (once)
--------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Install Python: https://www.python.org/downloads/macos/
     (big yellow Download button, then click through).
  2. Unzip VDM.zip and put the "VDM" folder in Downloads.
  3. Unblock it. macOS may say "Apple could not verify ... is
     free of malware" - click "Done" (NOT "Move to Trash").
     Then open Terminal (Cmd+Space, type Terminal), paste this
     and press Enter:
         xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/VDM
     (Or: System Settings > Privacy & Security > "Open Anyway".)
  4. Double-click "Start VDM.command". The first start takes
     about a minute, then VDM opens in your browser at
         http://127.0.0.1:5757

  KEEP THE TERMINAL WINDOW OPEN while you use VDM.
  Tip: drag "Start VDM.command" to your Dock.

--------------------------------------------------------------
 DOWNLOAD A VIDEO (YouTube, Instagram, Pinterest ...)
--------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Copy the video's link. In VDM click "Paste", then "Fetch".
  2. Choose Video (MP4) or Audio only (MP3), and the quality.
  3. Click "Download". When it says Done: "Open" or "Show in folder".

  Several at once: paste one link per line.
  Trim: tick "Trim" and set Start / End (like 1:30 and 2:45).
  More: subtitles, cover image, own file name, "Login from" for
  Instagram posts that need a login, "Save to" to pick a folder.


--------------------------------------------------------------
 DOWNLOAD MANAGER (like IDM) - any file, not just videos
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Click "Download Manager" at the top of VDM.

  - Add URL: paste any link (zip, pdf, exe, music, images ...).
    VDM splits the file into parts and downloads them at the same
    time, so it's faster. Video pages are downloaded as videos.
  - Resume / Stop: pause and continue later - even after closing
    VDM or restarting the computer.
  - Left side: Video, Music, Documents, Compressed, Programs,
    Images, Other - plus Unfinished, Finished, Queued, Failed.
  - Right-click a download: Open, Open folder, Redownload,
    Copy address, Delete, Delete with file.
  - Double-click a finished download to open it.
  - Drag a link from your browser into the window to add it.
  - Options: how many downloads run at once, connections per
    file, speed limit, and where to save.


--------------------------------------------------------------
 BROWSER ADD-ON (Download button on websites)
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Install: in VDM click "Install browser add-on" and follow the
  4 steps on screen (about 30 seconds, only once). When it's
  installed, VDM shows "Add-on installed" and the add-on opens
  by itself with a welcome page.

  What it does:
  - Purple "Download" button on YouTube, Instagram and Pinterest.
  - Streamed ("blob") videos on other sites: play the video, then
    click the VDM icon and press Download - all the pieces are
    joined into ONE complete MP4.
  - Takes over normal browser downloads (like IDM), so they get
    faster downloading and pause/resume. Your browser login is
    passed along, so downloads behind a login work too.
    (Only while VDM is running. Change it in the add-on's Settings.)
  - Right-click any link > "Download link with VDM (fast)".
  - Notifications when a download starts and when it finishes,
    with "Open" and "Show in folder" buttons.
  - Docks in the browser's side panel so it stays on screen.
    Turn this off in the add-on's Settings if you prefer a popup.
  - Shortcuts: Alt+Shift+V opens VDM, Alt+Shift+D downloads the
    video on the page you're looking at.
  - Pin it: click the puzzle piece in the toolbar, then the pin
    next to VDM.

  Firefox removes test add-ons when it closes - install it again
  after restarting Firefox. Chrome, Edge and Brave keep it.
  Safari: use the "VDM" bookmark link at the bottom of VDM.


--------------------------------------------------------------
 DOWNLOAD ON YOUR PHONE
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Your phone uses the computer to download, then saves the file.
  Phone and computer must be on the SAME Wi-Fi, VDM running.

  1. In VDM click "Use on phone" and tick
     "Allow my phone to use VDM".
  2. Scan the QR code with your phone's camera.
  3. On the phone: paste a link, tap Download, then
     "Save to phone".
  Tip: add the page to your phone's Home Screen.
  iPhone: you can share links straight from Instagram/YouTube -
  see "Tips for iPhone & Android" on the phone page.
  If the computer asks to allow incoming connections, click Allow.
  Only phones with the secret QR link can connect.


--------------------------------------------------------------
 NOTIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------
  In VDM click "Notifications: off" to turn them on, and allow
  them when the browser asks. The add-on has its own switches
  under Settings.


--------------------------------------------------------------
 PROBLEMS?
--------------------------------------------------------------
  "Apple could not verify" / "Move to Trash"
      Click Done, then run in Terminal:
         xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/VDM
      Or start it from Terminal (never blocked):
         cd ~/Downloads/VDM && bash start-mac-linux.sh
  "Operation not permitted"
      System Settings > Privacy & Security > Files & Folders >
      Terminal > turn on "Downloads Folder", quit Terminal
      (Cmd+Q) and start VDM again.
  "Python 3.10 or newer was not found" -> install Python (step 1).
  "CERTIFICATE_VERIFY_FAILED" -> Finder > Applications >
      Python 3.x > double-click "Install Certificates.command".
  Browser says "can't connect" to 127.0.0.1:5757
      -> VDM isn't running. Double-click "Start VDM.command".
  Video doesn't play in QuickTime -> keep "Plays in QuickTime /
      Preview" ticked. Fix old ones in Terminal:
         cd ~/Downloads/VDM && bash fix-old-videos-mac.sh
  Safari "Login from" -> System Settings > Privacy & Security >
      Full Disk Access > turn on Terminal.
  A site stopped working -> close Terminal and start VDM again
      (it updates itself).
  "Protected with DRM" -> Netflix, Prime Video, Hotstar,
      Disney+ etc. can't be downloaded.
  Phone can't connect -> same Wi-Fi? "Use on phone" switched on?
      Allow incoming connections if macOS asks.


Only download content you own or have permission to save.
