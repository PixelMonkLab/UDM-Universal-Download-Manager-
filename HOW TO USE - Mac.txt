==============================================================
  UDM - Universal DOWNLOAD Manager   v1.1.0 Beta
  HOW TO USE (MAC)
==============================================================


--------------------------------------------------------------
 INSTALL (once - no Python needed)
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Which download? Apple menu  > About This Mac:
    "Chip: Apple M1/M2/M3/M4..."  -> UDM-Mac-AppleSilicon.zip
    "Processor: Intel..."         -> UDM-Mac-Intel.zip

  1. Double-click the zip. Drag "UDM" into your Applications
     folder.
  2. Open it the first time like this (UDM isn't from the App
     Store yet, so macOS asks once):
       Right-click (or Control-click) UDM in Applications > Open >
       click "Open" again.
     If macOS only offers "Done" / "Move to Trash": click Done,
     then System Settings > Privacy & Security > scroll down >
     "Open Anyway" next to UDM.
     Says UDM "is damaged"? Open Terminal (Cmd+Space, type
     Terminal), paste this, press Enter, and open UDM again:
         xattr -cr /Applications/UDM.app
  3. After that, open UDM normally from Applications, Launchpad
     or Spotlight. Tip: right-click its Dock icon > Options >
     "Keep in Dock".

  UDM is a normal Mac app with its own window - no browser and no
  Terminal needed. Closing the window does NOT stop downloads:
  UDM keeps working in the menu bar (the UDM icon at the top
  right of the screen). Click UDM in the Dock or the menu bar
  icon to open the window again.

  QUIT UDM: Cmd+Q, or menu bar icon > "Quit UDM". Unfinished
  downloads are PAUSED and continue by themselves the next time
  you open UDM.
  Want UDM ready every time? Options > App > "Start UDM when I
  log in".


--------------------------------------------------------------
 DOWNLOAD A VIDEO (YouTube, Instagram, Pinterest ...)
--------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Copy the video's link and click "Video" in the toolbar
     (or Cmd+Shift+V). Click "Paste" - UDM shows the video.
  2. Choose Video (MP4) or Audio only (MP3/M4A/WAV), and quality.
  3. Click "Download". It appears in the list with its progress.

  Trim: tick "Trim" and drag Start / End (or type 1:30 and 2:45)
  to download only that part.
  Options: subtitles, cover image, cover art on music, own file
  name, all items of a carousel/playlist, and "Login from" for
  posts that need a login.
  Keep "Plays in QuickTime" ticked so videos open in QuickTime
  and Preview.


--------------------------------------------------------------
 DOWNLOAD MANAGER (like IDM) - any file, not just videos
--------------------------------------------------------------
  - Add URL (Cmd+N): paste any link (zip, pdf, dmg, music ...).
    UDM splits the file into parts and downloads them at the same
    time, so it's faster. Video pages are downloaded as videos.
    "Download later" puts it in the list paused.
  - Resume / Stop: pause and continue later - even after quitting
    UDM or restarting the Mac.
  - Left side: Video, Music, Documents, Compressed, Programs,
    Images, Other - plus Unfinished, Finished, Queued, Failed.
  - Click a download to see its details, including a map of the
    parts being downloaded at the same time.
  - Right-click a download: Open, Open folder, Resume, Stop,
    Redownload, Refresh download address, Copy address, Delete,
    Delete with file.
  - Double-click: opens a finished file, resumes a stopped one.
  - Drag a link from your browser onto the window to add it.
  - Search box above the list finds any download.
  - Options (Cmd+,): where to save, downloads at the same time,
    connections per file, speed limit, notifications.

  LINK EXPIRED? Some sites give download links that only work for
  a while. If one stops working, UDM asks for a fresh link by
  itself ("Refresh download address"), like IDM:
    - Easiest: click "Open the download page" and click the
      download link there again - the add-on hands it to UDM.
    - Or paste the new link and click "Use this link".
  Whatever was already downloaded is kept; UDM continues from
  where it stopped. UDM checks it's the same file first.


--------------------------------------------------------------
 BROWSER ADD-ON (Download button on websites)
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Install: click "Browser add-on" in UDM's toolbar, pick your
  browser and follow the 3 steps (about 30 seconds, only once).
  UDM shows "Add-on installed" when it's done.

  What it does:
  - Blue "Download" button on YouTube, Instagram and Pinterest.
  - Streamed ("blob") videos on other sites: play the video, then
    click the UDM icon and press Download - all the pieces are
    joined into ONE complete MP4.
  - Takes over normal browser downloads (like IDM), so they get
    faster downloading and pause/resume. Your browser login is
    passed along, so downloads behind a login work too.
    (Only while UDM is running. Change it in the add-on's Settings.)
  - Right-click any link > "Download link with UDM (fast)".
  - "Open UDM" in the add-on opens the UDM window.
  - Shortcuts: Alt+Shift+V opens UDM, Alt+Shift+D downloads the
    video on the page you're looking at.
  - Pin it: click the puzzle piece in the toolbar, then the pin
    next to UDM.

  Firefox removes test add-ons when it closes - install it again
  after restarting Firefox. Chrome, Edge and Brave keep it.


--------------------------------------------------------------
 DOWNLOAD ON YOUR PHONE
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Your phone uses the Mac to download, then saves the file.
  Phone and Mac must be on the SAME Wi-Fi, UDM running.

  1. Click "Phone" in UDM's toolbar and tick
     "Allow my phone to use UDM".
  2. Scan the QR code with your phone's camera.
  3. On the phone: paste a link, tap Download, then
     "Save to phone".
  Tip: add the page to your phone's Home Screen.
  If macOS asks to allow incoming connections, click Allow.
  Only phones with the secret QR link can connect.


--------------------------------------------------------------
 NOTIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------
  UDM shows a notification when a download starts and finishes.
  Turn them off in Options > App. If you see none, check System
  Settings > Notifications > UDM.


--------------------------------------------------------------
 PROBLEMS?
--------------------------------------------------------------
  "Apple could not verify" / "damaged" -> see INSTALL step 2.
  Nothing happens when I open UDM -> it's already running: click
      the UDM icon in the menu bar (top right).
  Add-on says "app not running" -> open UDM, then Retry.
  Instagram says "login required" -> set "Login from" to the
      browser you're logged in with.
  Safari "Login from" -> System Settings > Privacy & Security >
      Full Disk Access > add UDM.
  Old videos don't play in QuickTime -> download them again with
      "Plays in QuickTime" ticked.
  A site stopped working -> quit UDM (Cmd+Q) and open it again
      (it updates its video engine by itself).
  "Protected with DRM" -> Netflix, Prime Video, Hotstar,
      Disney+ etc. can't be downloaded.
  Phone can't connect -> same Wi-Fi? "Allow my phone" ticked?
      Allow incoming connections if macOS asks.
  Something else -> send the file  udm-desktop.log  from the
      hidden ".udm" folder in your home folder
      (Finder > Go > Go to Folder > ~/.udm).


Only download content you own or have permission to save.
