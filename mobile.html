Tips for iPhone & Android

Add to Home Screen: iPhone: Share ↑ → Add to Home Screen. Android: ⋮ → Add to Home screen.

Save a video to Photos (iPhone): tap Save to phone → Download. Then open the Files app → Downloads, tap the video, Share ↑ → Save Video.

Share straight from Instagram/YouTube (iPhone): make a Shortcut: Shortcuts app → + → "Receive URLs from Share Sheet" → add action Open URLs with:

followed by the Shortcut Input. Name it "VDM". Now in any app tap Share → VDM.

Your phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the computer, and VDM must be running there.
